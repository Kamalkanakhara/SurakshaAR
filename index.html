About the Solution

SurakshaAR is a scalable digital safety-training ecosystem for workers in industrial environments. The prototype shows how AR, mobile technology, multilingual content, assessments and digital certification can work together to improve the accessibility and management of safety training.

Proposed Technology Stack

Android app→AR engine (ARCore)→Local storage (Room)→Assessment→Certification→REST API→Database→Web admin

QR Verification Portal connects to the certification system. Kotlin, ARCore and Room are proposed, not implemented in this web prototype.

Built for Trust and Accountability

🔐 Worker authentication
🆔 Secure certificate IDs
🔳 QR verification
👤 Role-based admin access
🗂 Assessment records & audit history
🔄 Secure sync & expiry tracking
